About Us

Cape Tech Computer Solutions is a small IT business established in 2023 in Cape Town, South Africa.

The company provides affordable and reliable technology services to individuals, students and small businesses.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, affordable and reliable IT solutions that help individuals and businesses improve productivity through technology.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading small IT service providers in South Africa by delivering innovative technology solutions and outstanding customer service.
